Character Creation Rules

  1. You must join the Carniflora discord server and receive direct permission from species owner thescorpiondoctor in order to create or own a Carniflora character. Please ask species owner thescorpiondoctor for a server invite while the species is in early access. You may NOT create your own Carniflora characters if you are not in the official Carniflora discord server and have not been granted the Character Creation Pass role. Otherwise, you may commission thescorpiondoctor or a member of the species design team for a custom design or purchase an adoptable. Carniflora adoptables are available on thescorpiondoctor’s KoFi: https://ko-fi.com/thescorpiondoctor/shop/carniflora
  2. Upon being granted permission to create your own Carniflora character(s), you must adhere to the canon guidelines and limitations as well as follow the point buy system for powers and traits.
  3. While the species is in early access and until the proper species sheets and character creation guides have been finished, please send thescorpiondoctor your character design sketches before finalizing the drawing to ensure that the design fits the design guidelines of the species.
  4. When granted permission to create a character, you may only design Carniflora characters for yourself. You may not design characters for other people for free or for profit. Only Carniflora design team members may design customs for other Carniflora Community members. This rule is to keep character designs consistent with the design guidelines for the species.
  5. You must be over the age of 18 to access or distribute any 18+ or NSFW information or content surrounding this species. You MAY NOT distribute 18+ Carniflora content to anyone under the age of 18. Breaking this rule will result in a permanent ban from all Carniflora community spaces and your name going on a public blacklist. Keep all content sent in the main Carniflora server PG-13!
  6. All Carniflora designs must be approved by thescorpiondoctor before being made official. You must purchase an adoptable or MYO from thescorpiondoctor or the official species design team, design your own character, or commission thescorpiondoctor or someone on the species design team in order to acquire a Carniflora character for yourself. AI image generation, tracing, stealing, and copying designs for character creation are strictly prohibited within the Carniflora community.
  7. The only people allowed to make unlimited Carniflora character designs without trait and power limitations are the Carniflora Lore Team, Design Team, and people who have been granted explicit permission by thescorpiondoctor. Design and Lore team must still follow the power limitation rules and the point buy system.
